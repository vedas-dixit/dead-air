# Sound Link report

Generated by `tests/sound-link/report.test.ts` from in-memory runs. Do not edit by hand.
ggwave 0.4.0, 48000 Hz, volume 10. Each noise cell is 20 seeded random payloads sent once. White Gaussian noise is added to the whole stream, including the silence around the sound. The level is the ratio of the sound's power to the noise power over the whole band up to 24 kHz. ggwave's tones sit in a small part of that band, so it still decodes at ratios below 0 dB.

## What a real room still has to show

White noise is not a room. Echo, a phone speaker and a phone microphone change the sound in ways this test does not. A test with real speakers and phones is still needed (sound rules). Results go into the Field tests table in `docs/kiro-diary.md`.
